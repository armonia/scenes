The grammar

Twenty-six movements,
no cuts.

The vocabulary the scenes are built from. A transition is something you pick between two finished clips; a movement carries the film from one state to the next with no join to hide. Every entry here runs a live demo of the slab, states the numbers it starts from, and names the bench that can fail it.

Ten of the twenty-six name a bench that exists and runs in CI. The other sixteen name one that does not exist yet, and those are printed in grey: a measurement nobody has written is a proposal, and this page would be doing the thing it warns against if it let the two look alike.

seam.shA join that is really a cut in costume552 px against 18,019, 33×

fixture-screenshot.shMeasures nothing. It builds the scene the bench above has to fail, and without it that bench is a promisefirst frame magnified 2.26×

The negative control gets written first. A bench that cannot fail measures nothing, so it is made to exit non-zero on a broken input before its green is worth anything.
